## Mission previews

Generated as eight separate illustrations with the built-in ImageGen tool. The runtime images live in `assets/textures/missions/previews/`.

### Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one original mission preview illustration for the Mars Station Program pixel-art game, part of a matched set.
Style: premium 16-bit adventure-game background, actual crisp square pixel clusters, intentional two-to-four-tone shading per material, industrial hardware in ivory, dark indigo and rust, with a few amber or cyan accents. Rich atmosphere with restrained detail, strong silhouettes legible at 304 x 120.
Composition: full-bleed landscape, aim for 16:9. A wide horizontal scene with the complete focal subject centered within the middle 55 percent of the image height, so the top and bottom can be cropped for a 2.53:1 thumbnail. Build large readable shapes with foreground/middle/background. Keep the edges filled with the scene.

Constraints: one scene, opaque, no text, no letters, no labels, no graphic overlay, no panel border, no logo, no watermark, no anti-aliased vector edges, no photography or smooth 3D rendering.
