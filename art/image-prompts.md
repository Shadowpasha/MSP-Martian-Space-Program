# ImageGen production prompts

Both raster sources were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for Mars Station Program.

## Station

Use case: game environment raster background. Create original 16:9 landscape pixel art for the Mars Station Program main menu, composed to read at a 640×360 game grid. Wide cross-section through a lived-in industrial Mars station. Reserve the leftmost 25 percent as dark, quiet navy/charcoal station bulkhead for a separate game logo and vertical menu. Fill the right three quarters with two distinct horizontal zones: a tall panoramic slanted window across the upper half with vermilion Martian sunset, dusty layered purple and red ridges, a radar dish, a violet-domed greenhouse and a small solar array; below the window, a warm inhabited control deck with a wide recessed cyan monitor at center-right, green hydroponic shelves left of monitor, two small orange-suited crew sprites, exposed pipes, grilles, hazard stripes and lower mechanical service bay. Industrial navy structure and warm cream lamp highlights, careful clustered pixels, sharp 1990s premium pixel game background, selective cyan telemetry without text, controlled colors, subtle atmospheric perspective. Compose readable dominant shapes and distinct deck levels. The left quiet vertical quarter is required. No typography, no menus, no buttons, no UI frames or overlays, no logos, no watermark, no checkerboard, no photorealism or smooth painted illustration.

## Crew portraits

Create an original game sprite sheet, 3 columns by 2 rows with exactly six 16-bit pixel art astronaut busts for Mars Station Program. Use a plain single flat SOLID #E936B3 magenta chroma-key background behind every sprite, no shadow or texture on the magenta. The sheet edges divide into six equal rectangles, each portrait centered in its own rectangle with open magenta space on all sides. One portrait per box, all same level of scale, helmet size, upper-torso crop and chunky silhouette, shoulders forward in slight three-quarter view facing right. Characters in strict reading order top row: Elena Vance, tan-skinned brown-haired female Engineer in orange and off-white EVA hard suit with crossed-tool patch and wrench clipped shoulder; Kenji Sato, Japanese male Botanist in pale cream pressure suit with green utility hood and leaf patch; Maya Sterling, dark-skinned female Pilot with white flight helmet, smoky gold visor raised and rust-red pilots' jacket. Bottom row: Marcus Thorne, Black male Medic with warm brown skin, white suit, prominent red cross patch and white medic helmet; Hana Ishida, Japanese female Scientist with white scientific hood, two small cyan lab goggles and a blue tablet; Alex Keller, rugged middle-aged male Captain with a dark plum service cap with amber chevrons and navy command jacket over off-white suit. Sharp intentionally clustered pixels, industrial believable patched spacesuits, plum and navy shadow clusters, subtly warm light from upper left, minimal selective cyan highlights, fit for reducing each sprite to 64 by 80 pixels. Zero words, letters, frames, interface, labels, numbers, lines separating sprite cells or watermarks. Exactly six busts isolated on the flat magenta field.
